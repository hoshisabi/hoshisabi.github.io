# U-Con Charity Rerolls

**$1 = 1 reroll, and every dollar benefits Doctors Without Borders.**

Charity rerolls are back at every Adventurers League and Legends of Greyhawk table at U-Con 2026. Pick whichever way of giving is easiest for you:

## Donate online

Donate on our Doctors Without Borders page, then tell your DM how many rerolls you bought.

<div style="text-align: center; margin: 2rem 0;">
  <a class="lp-btn ghost" href="https://events.doctorswithoutborders.org/campaigns/PandoDnD">
    Donate to Doctors Without Borders →
  </a>
</div>

## Put cash on the table

Put $1 on the table for each reroll you want. Your DM will bring the donations to AL HQ.

## Get tickets at AL HQ

Stop by AL HQ to buy reroll tickets in advance, then bring them to any of your games.

## Bonus certificate for $5 or more

Donate $5 or more over the convention and you earn the **Toy Red Dragon** event certificate, an official Adventurers League award: a stuffed dragon that breathes a cone of fire once a day when squeezed. Pick it up at AL HQ.
