Elder chieftain of the Coldpeak. Has been watching people lie about the Netherese ruins in the mountain for thirty years. Smokes a pipe. Her name is Broken Tusk, and she has heard every joke about it.

She is the one who suggested bringing the party along when Kaarsk's hunters went missing, not because she trusts outsiders but because people who walked out of those ruins might be useful. She takes in information and decides what to do with it. Alina has been the party member she talks to most, and in [Session 15](../sessions/2026-08-08) Durok mentioned that Broken Tusk had spoken well of her research.

Her decisions aren't votes, but she doesn't give an order her people won't follow. When the Elk tribe asked to share Coldpeak's fire in [Session 16](../sessions/2026-08-21), Durok argued against it and the room agreed with him, until Alina asked what would happen if they killed the bird instead. Then it became a hunt, and the camp sent hunters with the party.

The Elk are coming to her now as the Tribe of the Weaver, sixty of them, led by [Ragnhild](ragnhild). The two have kept an understanding between their peoples for many seasons, and now they'll share a camp.
