The youngest volunteer in the war party that marched northwest to reach the Elk tribe. His first expedition beyond Coldpeak's hunting perimeter. Fast — the fastest runner in the column — and trying not to show how eager he was. Asked Berg about formation on the march out.

When the forced dash through Brotherhood fog pushed everyone past their limits, Tarn hit exhaustion and fell just slightly behind the rest. Rime Talon descended from the clouds and took him. Grath, who had walked beside him the whole way, said nothing.

*First and last appeared during Session 17.*
