A mages' organization out of Luskan, working the edges of the Ten-Towns and the ruins beyond them. The party met the Brotherhood before they understood it as a threat — first as researchers, then as a name behind the things that have been circling Cold Peak.

What the party has seen up close is an organization that runs on competition and secrecy. Its members guard their sources, withhold their findings, and tend to treat generosity as leverage poorly spent. They are interested in the crystals, the Netherese ruins, and the dead — and in anything recovered from the archaeologists who went missing in the area.

## The people the party has met

[**Thessaly Vorn**](../npcs/thessaly) is a junior field researcher out of Luskan, and the exception that proves the rule — she left the party a genuine token of invitation rather than a calculated one, and she is the sort who answers a letter honestly even when a smarter political actor wouldn't. She took [Renier's journal](../items/renier-journal) for safekeeping. A senior name from Luskan has since taken an interest in her field report.

[**Aldric**](../npcs/aldric) is her companion and protector — not a hired sword, something closer than that. Where the Brotherhood is transactional, he is the person who makes sure Thessaly comes home.

[**Sarevyn Cole**](../npcs/sarevyn-cole) leads the Brotherhood's expedition at the hunting lodge outside Caer-Konig. He is the Brotherhood the party had been warned about: charm deployed with decades of practice, curiosity that is genuine and self-serving at the same time. He offered Alina an Enduring Spellbook in exchange for whatever had been recovered from the missing archaeologists — framed as returning their effects to their families. The insight roll said that wasn't the only reason.

## The compass rose — the northern chapter

For two sessions the party kept finding the same mark: a compass rose whose north point is larger and bolder than the others. It was on the wolf collars worn by the constructs last month. It was carved into the gems recovered from the ice elementals that harried an Elk scout across the snow. Alina's Arcana put it together — the mark is the Brotherhood's, and the emphasis on the north point is the signature of its **northern chapter**.

That reframed everything the camp had been enduring. Six harrying attacks had reached Cold Peak while the party was away — mephits, giant snowy owls, a dozen creatures or fewer each time, never quite enough to break the defenses. Read together, they weren't killing attacks. They were pressure: directed from the northwest, toward Caer-Konig, meant to make the ground under the Cold Peak tribe feel unsafe and to push the tribe rather than finish it. The Elk tribe has been feeling the same pressure — and, by Ulfe's account, something worse moving in the same direction, the ice-and-wind bird his people have started calling Rime Talon.

The party does not yet know what the northern chapter wants from this stretch of the Dale, only that it wants them moved, and that it has been willing to spend constructs, elementals, and worse to do it.
