A round, dense melon from Chult, and the last thing anyone expects to find in a frozen apocalypse. Cut one open and the exposed face radiates actual heat — amber-lit, wisps of warmth rising off it, warm enough to feel across a cold stone table. It is fruit that fights the cold on its own terms, which is exactly why [Durok Deepseeker](../npcs/durok) has spent so much effort on it.

Durok grows pembelon in the enclosed hot ground of [his volcanic oasis](../locations/duroks-spring), where the warmth of the earth does the work the sky refuses to. There it thrives. The unsolved problem — the one he half-hoped the party's Arcana might crack — is getting it to survive out in the open snow, away from the heat of the spring. That is the whole project: a crop that could feed people who don't happen to live on top of a warm anomaly. He arrived at Cold Peak with canvas bags of them, food grown against the season, offered plainly to a camp that has been stretched thin.

## The concentrate

The same crop-hardiness research produced a side effect Durok did not set out to make: a concentrated vial of something so aggressively foul that it functions as a weapon of its own. It smells horrible. That is the entire description, and it does not do it justice.

[Brekk](../npcs/brekk) decided immediately that this was the funniest thing in the camp, which is high praise from the tribe's foremost authority on [gunting](gunting). "Hey, smell this." What is it? "It smells horrible." Three of the party waved it under their noses and failed their saves for it; [River](../characters/river) failed, and then tried again, which somehow made it funnier. Only [Alina](../characters/alina) watched the whole performance and declined. Durok's serious agricultural research and Brekk's sense of humor turned out to be, briefly, the same object.
