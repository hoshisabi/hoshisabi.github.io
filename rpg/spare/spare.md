## Campaign: Icewind Dale — Rime of the Frostmaiden (Heroes That We Need)
### DM: Chris Hubbard — in-person — (Retired)

Spare looks human, though his lineage is Shadovar. What he actually is takes longer to explain.

Back in Netheril, an academy instructor lost a student who reached past his skill and died for it.
The boy was a middle child from a minor noble house, overlooked and unremarkable — the family's
own "spare." The academy paid the right people, the family accepted the settlement, and the matter
was closed. The instructor never quite let it go. Working in secret, he tried to bring the student
back and managed only a partial clone.

When Karsus's Folly brought down Netheril, the instructor died with it, and his clone vats failed
one by one — all except this one. The half-finished boy remained suspended in stasis for millennia
until Velene uncovered the vat and woke him into servitude, with no idea what he was.

He later stumbled out into Icewind Dale in clothes meant for a warmer age, carrying no memory of
how he got there. A family of fishermen pulled him from the cold and kept him alive. *(Chris's
addition to his backstory.)*

Spare is neither the instructor nor the student, just an unfinished piece of both. He carries
tangled fragments of their memories and the quiet, frustrating certainty that he ought to know far
more magic than he can cast — a low-level wizard trying to remember how to be a Netherese one.
Neither of their real names survived, so "Spare" stuck.

He traveled with the party through the early chapters of *Rime of the Frostmaiden*, acting as
eyes and ears out of the mines and reporting to Sheriff Southwell, until a confrontation with
Father Lake left him spent. He went back to Velene, refused to continue her work, and was killed
for it.

*The story continued without him.*

**Build:** Wizard 4 (Order of Scribes) — INT 18, spell save DC 14, +7 spell attack. Wizardly Quill,
Awakened Spellbook, Arcane Recovery.

**Feat:** Shadow Touched (*Invisibility* and *Silent Image*).

**Notable equipment:**
- *Wand of the War Mage, +1* (attuned)
- Awakened spellbook
- Cold-weather clothing (from the fishermen who found him)

**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral · **Languages:** Abyssal, Common, Draconic, Infernal
