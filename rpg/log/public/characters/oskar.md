## Introduction

This is **Oskar**, a human from **Furyondy** and a blacksmith's son. He wasn't the one set to inherit the smithy. He was too full of himself and a menace to everyone around him, until a wandering priest of **St. Cuthbert** knocked some sense into him. Literally. He was a changed man afterwards. Maybe it knocked something loose.

Now he's an acolyte of St. Cuthbert of the Cudgel, and he spreads the faith the way it reached him: percussive persuasion, concussive charisma, blunt beguilement.

Oskar is a **3rd-level fighter, a Champion**, though he's convinced he's a healer. To be fair, he carries a healer's kit and knows a prayer or two. He's just as likely to patch you up as to tell you to rub some dirt on it and get back in the fight.

*"St. Cuthbert opened my mind. With a cudgel. I'm happy to return the favor."*
