## Introduction

This is **Logos**, a grey elf from the **Valley of the Mage**. Yes, *that* valley. No, he doesn't work for him.

Other elves don't quite trust anyone from the valley, so Logos doesn't pray to Corellon. He's an acolyte of **Boccob the Uncaring**, the god of magic, who doesn't care where you're from. For Logos, that's the whole appeal.

He's a **3rd-level wizard, a Diviner**. He's tall and pale with black hair, and a hundred and eighteen years old, which is young for an elf. To him the universe is a proof, and magic is the part of the math most people haven't finished reading.

The owl is **Phi**, named for the golden ratio. Phi does the scouting and Logos does the arithmetic.

His weakness: give him three good options and a time limit, and he'll ask for a fourth option, which is more time.

*"I don't believe in luck. I believe in probabilities I haven't calculated yet."*
