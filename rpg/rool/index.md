## Campaign: Wild Beyond the Witchlight
### DM: Kate Hoff — in-person — (Finished)

Rool is a fairy druid loosely modeled on the brownies from *Willow*: small, bearded, winged, and
far more capable than his size suggests. Before the campaign, he and another member of his faerie
band rescued a lost human infant. Everyone back home called him a hero for it. He felt nothing —
no pride, no satisfaction, just an empty space where accomplishment was supposed to be, paired
with a habit of making strangers regard him with polite pity rather than actual respect.

He went looking for something big enough to finally feel like he had earned the praise, which led
him back to the Witchlight Carnival and through it into Prismeer. Along the way he gathered an
odd assortment of carnival prizes and fey keepsakes, recovered his lost thing in Loomlurch
(*Rool's Mantle of the Forest Guardian*), and saw the party all the way to the Palace of Heart's
Desire — where, under pressure to share a secret, he was forced to admit that not all of pocket
chicken is chicken.

**Build:** Druid 11 (Circle of the Shepherd) — WIS 20, spell save DC 18, +10 spell attack.
Summoner and spirit caller (Spirit Totem, Mighty Summoner, Guardian Spirit; Wild Shape up to CR 1).

**Notable equipment:**
- *Moon Sickle, +1* (attuned)
- *Rool's Mantle of the Forest Guardian* — homebrew cloak (+3 AC), recovered as his lost thing in Loomlurch
- Flask of glow worms that shift color with his mood (inconvenient for a private fairy)
- Tiny pumpkin-shaped cauldron of bog oak (won at Snail Racing)
- Wizard hand puppet (*Detect Magic*, won at Sea Shell Soufflé)
- Fist-sized floating rock (won at Feywild Trivia)
- Wooden box of baby teeth (won at Goat Jousting)
- Pixie dust

**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral · **Languages:** Common, Druidic, Halfling, Sylvan
