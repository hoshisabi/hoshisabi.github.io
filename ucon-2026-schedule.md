## Reading the grid

- **Initials** are the DM scheduled for that table (organizer shorthand, not a full roster).
- Blank cells mean that adventure is not scheduled in that slot.
- **Epic** adventures may list several DMs in one cell — they run as a coordinated multi-table event.
