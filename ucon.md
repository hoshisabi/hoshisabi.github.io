## Signing Up to Play

Registration is open on [Tabletop.Events](https://tabletop.events/conventions/u-con-2026), and tables are filling as badges sell. The [schedule grid]({{ '/ucon-2026-schedule/' | relative_url }}) links each table straight to its event page.

---

## Charity

Every table offers charity rerolls benefiting **Doctors Without Borders**. Donations are collected at AL HQ during the convention.

---

## Health & Safety

U-Con's official health and safety policies are at [ucon-gaming.org/policies](https://www.ucon-gaming.org/policies/). We ask everyone to prioritize their health and the health of those around them. If you're feeling unwell, please stay home. No table is worth getting people sick, and we'd much rather sort out coverage than have anyone push through it.
